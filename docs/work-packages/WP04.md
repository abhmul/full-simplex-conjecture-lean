`PairWeights.lean` compiles warning-clean with 18 named theorems. `q_symm` proves symmetry of the single frozen definition, and `q_nonneg` is unconditional. `stdGaussian_isOpenPosMeasure` combines the scalar Gaussian's public absolute-continuity theorem, product open positivity and the actual Pi-to-Euclidean Gaussian map. `multivariateGaussian_pos_of_mem_open` then proves positive mass for every open neighborhood of the mean even for singular covariance (and the total covariance fallback). `pairSuccess_pos_of_mean_lt` uses this theorem on the strict remaining-coordinate event. Thus general row positivity precedes PD exclusion as required.
