## Independent product-law sum

`FSC/Gaussian/IndependentSum.lean` proves scalar transport, independent addition, arbitrary real weighted independent sums with arbitrary means, and the centered `G + s • L` law for s ≥ 0. The measure being mapped is the actual product of the two Gaussian laws, so independence is established by construction and not inferred from moments. Both G and L are arbitrary PSD matrices; the endpoint s=0 and rank-zero L are included.

The scalar image is a consumer of the proven rectangular affine infrastructure. The additive convolution is identified through public `Measure.ext_of_charFun`, `charFun_conv` and `charFun_multivariateGaussian`; its Gaussian target and explicit covariance are therefore identified by equality of the full characteristic functions.
