# Current implementation state

The formalization implements the exact [contract](PUBLIC_CONTRACT.md): the comparison in every dimension and the equality case at positive thresholds. Every acceptance check has passed. The certified source is commit `1915b3485c8d61e531374261e7844010b7538eb9`. See the [verification report](RELEASE_AUDIT.md) and the [source-equivalence receipt](../checks/release/source-equivalence.json).

The public import is `FSC`. The accepted declarations, all in namespace `FSC`, are `cdf_simplex_le`, `cdf_eq_simplex_iff`, `cdf_simplex_lt`, `lowerOrthant_simplex_le`, `coordinateMax_tail_le_simplex`, `coordinateMax_tail_lt_simplex`, and `coordinateMax_tail_eq_simplex_iff`. The comparison covers every $n\ge2$, every correlation matrix, and every real threshold. In the equality and strictness theorems, the only condition on the threshold is that $t>0$ is fixed. Every analytic input specific to this project is proved. The internal assumptions of the proof (distinct coordinates, a matrix that is not positive definite, a minimizer, and the induction hypothesis) are all discharged. No unfinished mathematical input remains.

All 28 [work packages](WORK_PACKAGES.md) are integrated. Following the support/noise route of [ARCHITECTURE.md](ARCHITECTURE.md), the formalization proves:

- the Gaussian laws for every positive-semidefinite covariance, singular ones included;
- the calculus of Gaussian measures of polyhedra as functions of their offsets (the support calculus), with redundant constraints allowed;
- the second-order Peano expansion in the thresholds, with the Hessian identified through the canonical pair weights $q$;
- the right derivative along added noise, with each coordinate rescaled to unit variance;
- a positive pair weight in every row, without assuming singularity;
- the energy calculation in the full dimension $n-2$, unused Gaussian directions included;
- the exclusions at a minimizer (repeated coordinates and positive-definite matrices) and the rigidity of minimizers;
- the induction on $n$, which uses only the lower-dimensional comparison; and
- the barrier argument over a compact interval of thresholds.

The regression tests permanently keep the independent calculation for the singular triangle (a $3\times3$ correlation matrix of rank two), along with the checks that apply the general derivative to that triangle and to a $5\times5$ example of rank at most two. The tests explicitly cover repeated and antipodal coordinates, reference offset vectors with ties, redundant constraints, unbounded regions, and empty constraint families. They also exhibit two failures. Under the law with two coordinates pinned at the threshold, the boundary of the full lower orthant, pinned coordinates included, is not a null set ([PairFrontierAdverse.lean](../checks/release/PairFrontierAdverse.lean)). At a nonpositive threshold, equality holds for a matrix other than the simplex ([checks/wp21/Adverse.lean](../checks/wp21/Adverse.lean)).
